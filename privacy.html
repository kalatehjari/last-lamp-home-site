Last Lamp Home: Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026 · applies to the Last Lamp Home apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux

In short: Last Lamp Home collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Everything it saves stays on your device.

Who we are

Last Lamp Home is made by Roohollah Kalatehjari, an independent developer ("we"). Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact link at the end of this page.

What the app saves on your device

The game keeps a small amount of information in the app's own storage on your device, so that it can remember things between sessions:

  • Player profiles: the player names you type in, best scores, number of games, kills, the furthest street reached and the outfits you pick.
  • The local scoreboard: your recent runs.
  • Settings: sound, quality, camera, flash effects, Salty Talk and similar options.
  • Bestiary progress: which creatures you have met.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Player names are just labels for the local scoreboard. You don't need to use your real name.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It makes no network connections. The game, its fonts and its sounds are all inside the app.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Device features

On a phone, the app can vibrate (haptic feedback) during big hits. You can turn this off with Vibration in the pause menu. If you connect a game controller, the app reads its buttons only while you play. Neither feature collects or stores any data.

Screenshots

Photo mode freezes the game so you can take a screenshot with your device's own screenshot function. The app itself does not save or upload images.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or clear its storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Last Lamp Home → Storage → Clear storage), and everything it saved is removed. On a computer, uninstall the game and delete its data folder (Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/Last Lamp Home; Windows: %APPDATA%\Last Lamp Home; Linux: ~/.config/Last Lamp Home). Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Last Lamp Home is meant for players aged 13 and over. It contains fantasy violence and horror themes. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
