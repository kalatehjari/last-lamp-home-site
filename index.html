Last Lamp Home

A 3D beat-'em-up painted in watercolour: three friends, one long walk home through a spirit town at 2 AM. For iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux.

Need help? Open an issue at github.com/kalatehjari/last-lamp-home-site/issues. Please say which device you play on and which version (it's at the top right of the title screen).

Common questions

  • How do I play on a phone? Drag the left ring to walk; double-tap it to jump and triple-tap to crouch. Push the right ring toward an action (punch, kick, uppercut, sweep, special, laser, fire, flash) and tap to use it. HOW TO PLAY on the title screen explains everything.
  • The game is slow or warm on my phone. Choose SD on the title screen or in the pause menu (II). The game also lowers its graphics by itself if the frame rate stays low.
  • Can I use a controller? Yes. Connect it in your device's Bluetooth settings: left stick moves, A jump, X punch, B kick, Y special.
  • Too many flashes? Pause, then turn FLASH EFFECTS to REDUCED.
  • Where are my scores? On your device only. The game has no accounts and never goes online.
